Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 7, 2026

Welcome to BusBuddy PRO ("the Application"), a smart school transportation tracking utility developed and operated by Himpower Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

By downloading, accessing, or using BusBuddy PRO, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

In order to provide our real-time tracking and communication services, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Location Data (Critical Service): We collect precise real-time geographic location data from devices acting under the "Teacher/Driver" role while their shifts are active. This data is used to render the bus marker on the map for parents and school administrators. We do NOT track drivers when they are off-shift.
  • Account & Registration Data: To connect with a school system, we require verification via a school code. We process role selections (Driver, Parent, Admin) and associated metadata.
  • Communications & Chat Logs: Any text messages, announcements, or status updates sent via the in-app chat system are securely processed and stored in our database so that participants can read them.
  • Device & Usage Information: We may collect basic diagnostic, performance, and usage data to maintain and troubleshoot application stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and update real-time school bus location tracking services.
  • To facilitate in-app communication (announcements, parent messages, status indicators) between drivers/teachers and parents.
  • To enable safety features, such as triggering and distributing emergency SOS notifications.
  • To secure the platform against unauthorized access using Firebase authentication.
  • To comply with regulatory obligations and official security checks.

3. Data Storage, Security, and Protection

We leverage industry-standard Firebase (Cloud Firestore) to process and store all application data securely. We enforce strict database security rules ensuring that only authenticated users belonging to a school group can write or read associated data. We make reasonable efforts to protect your personal data, but please be aware that no security system is 100% secure.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Your privacy is paramount. We do NOT sell, lease, or trade your personal or location data to third parties. Data is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With school administrators and associated parents belonging to your verified school system to ensure student safety.
  • With service providers (such as Google Maps API for rendering geographic features) in accordance with their respective privacy policies.
  • If required by law, court order, or governmental authorities to ensure public safety or protect legal rights.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you or request its complete deletion from our systems. If you wish to delete your account, your boarding history, or any communication records, please contact us at the email address listed below.
